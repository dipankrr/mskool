Staff list, redesigned

The current list puts a green “Joined” pill on almost every row, shows employee codes nobody reads, and says nothing about what a teacher actually does. This version stays quiet when someone is fine, flags people only when they need something, and shows each teacher's subjects and classes. Mockup only; no app code has changed.

What changes
All · Teaching · OfficeTeaching = "Teaches classes" is on. Office = the title isn't a teacher title (the same check the Add staff form uses). Someone can be in both. A guess from the title; a role field would make it exact.
Show people who leftA switch at the end of the list, off by default, hidden when nobody has left. Covers resigned, retired and let go.
Status only when it matters"Joined" disappears. An amber dot on the photo and a pill appear only for Invited or Not invited. The Invite card on top opens just those people.
What they teachSubject and sections for each teacher, ★ for the class they lead. Needs the staff list to return this; the Teaching overview already has it.
Call buttonThe most common reason to open this list is to reach someone. No phone, no button.
Codes off the rowEmployee codes move to the profile. Search still finds them.
