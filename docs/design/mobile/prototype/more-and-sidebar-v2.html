More tab and sidebar, v2

Three surfaces, one job each. More gives the big areas that have no tab a proper front door. The sidebar is the web sidebar on a phone: every screen and quick action, grouped the same way, opened from the menu button. The account popup opens from the avatar and holds who you are, your branch and session, and sign out. On the first phone, tap the menu button or the avatar.

More

  • Three showcase cards: Exams, Academics, Staff. Each shows its state and three doors into it.
  • Two smaller cards for Timetable (not built yet) and School.
  • Nothing else. No setup list, no chips, no search, no account rows. Those live in the sidebar and the account popup.
  • Warnings sit on the card they belong to (the amber "3 gaps" under Teaching), not in a separate strip.

Sidebar

  • Same groups as web: Daily, Academics, Administration. Same fold-open items.
  • Actions are green with a plus (Admit a student, Collect a fee). Plain lines are places.
  • It repeats the tabs. Students, Attendance, Homework and Fees are here too, so it is the complete map.
  • "On web" marks screens the phone does not have. "Soon" marks ones not built anywhere.
  • Filtered by permission, item by item, like web.
  • Opens from the menu button only. No edge swipe.

Account popup

  • Opens from the avatar, top right, on every tab.
  • Name, roles, Profile and access, Change password.
  • Branch and session switchers move here from More.
  • Sign out.
  • Viewing a past session turns the session block amber, with one tap back.

Open for you to decide

  • Which areas earn a showcase card. I drew Exams, Academics, Staff. Photos and ID cards? Reports?
  • Mobile-only extras in the sidebar. Homework, Teaching, Photo day and Family logins are not in the web sidebar. I added them; web would then need to catch up or differ.
  • Screens marked "On web": list them on the phone, or hide them until built?
  • Menu button on every tab, beside each tab's own header? Attendance hides its header on scroll.
  • Screen search in the sidebar: now, later, or never?
