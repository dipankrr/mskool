Tab bar, from scratch

The white capsule disappears because it is white on white with nothing that says you are here. These five ideas start from what the app already has: Clash, the floating capsule, and the area colours every screen uses (green attendance, teal students, purple homework, blue fees). Tap the tabs in the phone to move around the app, and scroll a screen to see how the bar behaves.

Pick the screen that worries you most. Students is the white list where today's bar vanishes. Attendance puts a green Save button right above the bar.

Where I'd go

Wayfinder (1) is the one I'd build. Today the selected tab is a 12% wash; here it is a solid pill in the colour of the area you are in, sliding between tabs. It fixes visibility on every backdrop, and the bar starts doing a job: it tells you which part of the school you are in, in the same colour as that screen's icons and buttons.

  • Add Steps aside (5) on top if you want the long lists to breathe: it is Wayfinder plus scroll behaviour, and it replaces today's hide-on-scroll.
  • Night island (3) is the boldest and the easiest to see, but it is the only dark object in a light app.
  • Ground (2) is the quietest, but it tints the bottom of every screen.
  • Build: one Reanimated value for the pill's position, colour from the existing area tokens. No new libraries except blur for Lens on Android.
