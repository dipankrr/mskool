Where I'd go

If I had to move only two things: Top colour a touch lighter than today (it reads as glass, not as a hole) and a little gloss on the top half, which catches light like a real pane. That is the "Lit" preset.

  • Check Matte too: it is what the bar looks like with no blur, which is what a slow Android phone will get. If Matte looks fine, the glass is a bonus, not a risk.
  • Keep top opacity above about 0.65 on the Students screen, or the white list starts to wash the bar out.
  • Everything here maps to the app: the gradient to expo-linear-gradient, the blur to expo-blur, the edges to boxShadow.
