Homework, for students

The family app's homework, in the flat style you picked: soft ground, grey rounded rows, heavy type, black pills, Bookie and handwritten notes. Every homework shows one of your six words: To do, Sent, Late, Checked, Redo, Missed. Each word has its own colour and is used everywhere. All of it reads from portalHomework.*, which is already built; the streak and the "tonight" count are worked out on the phone.

Choices in this mockup

  • Streak: "on time in a row", counting sends and ticks. It never shames: when it breaks it just disappears, with no "you lost your streak". Keep it, or drop all game parts?
  • Stamp numbers ("No. 07") count this term's sent work. It's a small collectible feel. Keep or drop?
  • "Next: …" after sending chains the evening. Alternative: always go back to the list.
  • Parents see the same screens (parent = student view), with the buttons kept. A parent can send photos for a young child. Or should parents only look?
  • Quiz results: released by the teacher (as above), or shown right after submitting? Your rule says results show "on save" for checked work, and quizzes are graded automatically.
  • A ticked homework shows "Done" here, which isn't one of the six words. Use "Sent", or add "Done" as a seventh word just for ticks?
  • Late sending: after the due time the button stays and the word becomes Late. Should teachers be able to stop late work for a homework?
