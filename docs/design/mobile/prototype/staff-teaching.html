Staff, sign-in and teacher assignment

A mockup for review, with no app code changed. It follows the design doc 06-staff-and-teaching.md: one kind of teacher, phone sign-in with a joining code, and one place to say who teaches what. Assigning a teacher is what lets them work, so these screens set up marks, homework, attendance and Home for every teacher. Names and numbers are made up for the mockup (Main Campus, session 2026-27, 20 sections).

What the screens are built around
One kind of teacherStaff are just "Teacher". What they teach, and whether they look after a section as its class teacher, is on the assignment, not on the person.
Assigning is the accessPut Priya in 6-A Maths and she can open 6-A Maths. There's no Roles step for teachers any more.
Gaps are always visibleEvery teaching screen counts what's empty. Empty slots are amber and say Assign, so the office always knows what's left.
Phone and a code, no emailThe office adds the teacher and shares a 6-digit code on WhatsApp. The teacher sets their own password and finds their classes waiting.
Subjects you can spotEach subject gets a soft tile with an icon, so a section's eight subjects read at a glance. It's the first non-status colour in the staff app (open question 1).
Leaving hands overMarking someone as left lists everything they taught, so nothing goes silently unstaffed.
