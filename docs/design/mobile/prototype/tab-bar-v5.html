Smoked glass, eight ways

The smoked glass bar from v4, pushed in eight directions: what the selected tab looks like, whether names show, the tint of the glass, and the shape of the capsule. All of them keep the same dark glass, so any detail can be mixed into another. Tap the tabs, scroll the screens, and try the fold switch under the phone.

All eight, same screen

Pick the screen that worries you most. Students is the white list where today's bar vanishes. Attendance puts a green Save button right above the bar.

Where I'd go

Porcelain pill (2) if staff should never wonder where they are, Halo rim (7) if it should feel the most premium. Both keep the names under the icons, which matters for teachers who are new to the app. Mint (4) mixes into either if you want a touch of the brand.

  • Unfold (3) and Slim (8) drop the names. They look cleaner but put more on the icons.
  • Split (6) narrows the five main tabs to make room for the More button.
  • Android blur still means adding expo-blur. On phones too slow for it, the same bar turns solid, and every version here still works without the blur.
