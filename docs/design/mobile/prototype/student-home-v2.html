Student Home v2: the daily loop

One Home, shown in two halves because it scrolls. The evening goes top to bottom: Bookie says one line, Tonight lists the work and the bag, Tomorrow shows the day ahead, and Today at school tells what happened. Tick things in the first phone and watch Bookie. Switch the day to see how Home changes.

What Bookie says

One line at a time, picked by the most pressing thing. The face changes with it. This is the only playful piece in this round.

For both readersCopy never says "you" or "your child", so it reads right for a student and for a parent on the same phone.
Works with what existsHomework rows, the bag items that come from bring-to-class homework, attendance, checked work with the teacher's note, fees and the exam schedule.
Needs new backendTomorrow's periods (timetable is designed, not built), "what the class did" (a class diary), exam marks on Home (results are still out of scope) and notices.
Bag ticks stay on the phoneTicking a bag item is a reminder for the family only. Nothing is sent to the teacher.
Not in this roundStreak, stamps, confetti and a results reveal. Sign out moves off Home to a profile page behind the name chip.
OpenThe five tabs are unchanged here. Whether Attendance, Exams and Fees still each deserve a tab is a separate question.
