Roles, status and the rest of staff management

Today the phone can add a librarian or a principal, but it can't give them access: the job title is only text, and roles are given on web. This mockup adds one question to Add staff, “What do they do here?”, and a Roles card on the profile. Teacher is one of the answers, but it gives no role: it marks them as teaching, and their classes come from Teaching, section by section, as now. Frames R10 to R19 cover what else is missing in staff management: on leave and suspended, changing a joined person's phone, moving branch, removing a photo, history, roles that end, export, and reading what a role can do. Leave requests (apply, approve, balances) are left for their own design. Mockup only; no app code has changed. Builds on staff-teaching.html (frames 06, 06b, 08, 14 and 18) and the design note in 06-staff-and-teaching.md §11.

Questions for you
1. Job title and the picks. R2 fills the title from the first office pick and lets you change it.
A · Fill it, keep it editable. Fewer taps, and “Principal” in the list matches the title. A school that writes “Head Mistress” has to edit it once.
B · Keep them separate. The title is only what's printed. Clearer, but people will pick Principal and also type “Principal”.
2. What a role can do, on the phone. Changing it needs role_permission:update (org admin by default).
A · Web only. R9 lists who holds what. Least work; changing a role is rare.
B · Read-only list on the phone. Tap a role in R9 to see what it can do, in plain words. Editing stays on web.
C · The full editor on the phone (R20 to R24). Groups, chips per action, a review step before saving, the two locks shown up front, and reset. All the server calls exist; it's phone work only. The most work of the three, and a wrong tap changes access for everyone with the role, which is what R22's review is for.
3. A role before they join. A role belongs to a login today, so R2 and R5 need the role kept with the staff record until the person joins, as Teaching already does. Is “Starts when he joins” the right behaviour, or should roles be offered only after they join?
4. “Other staff” wording. R1 says Other staff; R3 says Office staff. Pick one name for a person with no role.
5. Sign-in while on leave.
A · Keeps signing in. She can still see her classes and messages; covers do the work. Simplest.
B · Blocked like a suspension. Nothing gets done in her name while she's away, but she can't check anything either.
6. A joined person's new phone.
A · Switch at once (R13). The old number stops on save; she needs the new code to sign in again.
B · Old number works until she joins with the new one. No lock-out, but for a while the record and the sign-in disagree, which is what happens today without a warning.
7. Which of these first. The suspension fix (R12) and the phone warning (R13) fix things that are wrong today. The rest are new features.
What the server needs
Already there: role.assign, role.revoke (asks for a reason), role.assignments, role.holders, staff.reactivate. Giving a role checks role_assignment:assign at the branch or organisation it's given for, so a branch holder can give only for that branch, and Org admin needs it at organisation level. Teacher roles by hand are refused. Leaving already takes every role away.
New: roles waiting for someone who hasn't joined, kept against the staff record and given when they join, like Teaching's grants. Add staff sends its picks with the create call, so saving stays one step.
Wrong today: a suspension keeps the sign-in and every role (staff.service switches off only on leaving). Editing a joined person's phone leaves the sign-in on the old number (read from the code, not walked). on_leave counts as current but nothing can set it.
New calls: set on leave and back; lift a suspension (block sign-in on suspend, give access back on lift); move branch; roles by end date and extend; one person's history from authz_audit_log; staff export. Already there: staff.removePhoto, role.permissions.
To check at build time: how the phone knows the caller can give org-wide roles (for the Org admin choice and the branch switch). It may already follow from the permissions me returns.
