Welcome screen

The first screen before sign-in. It takes the feel of the reference (Bookie as the hero, a bold headline, a little play) and swaps the Get started button for two role cards: Student / Parent opens the family sign-in, Staff / Teacher opens the staff sign-in. Three directions to react to. Words are placeholders.

When it showsProposal: only when the phone has no remembered sign-in choice (first launch, or after signing out). Otherwise staff would tap a card every time they open the app. Each sign-in form gets a back arrow to return here.
BookieSame drawing and motion as the app's Bookie component: slow sway, blink every few seconds, both off under Reduce Motion.
Mix and matchThe parts move between directions freely: A's blue stage with B's card subtitles, C's side-by-side tiles on a light screen, and so on.
