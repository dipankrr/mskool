Things mSkool will never let anyone do.

Some rules live in the software, not in the staff handbook.

Records

Delete a student, a payment or a day's attendance.

Records are kept for good. Mistakes are corrected, never erased.

Never
Fees

Change a fee receipt after it is issued.

A refund is a new entry, so the books always add up and your auditor can follow every rupee.

Never
Receipts

Skip or reuse a receipt number.

Each branch counts up in order, the way receipt books always did.

Never
Results

Show marks to families before they're published.

The principal decides when results go out.

Never
Access

Let staff into a branch they don't work in.

A subject teacher sees their sections. An accountant sees fees. Nothing more.

Never

Notices parents can actually read.
